About Karat

Karat is a simple gold jewellery calculator that helps you estimate the value of a gold piece based on its karat, weight, gold price, making charges, and VAT.

Reporting an issue

When contacting support, please include as much detail as possible so we can understand the issue.

  • Your operating system version
  • What happened
  • A screenshot, if relevant
